ECP delegator (aruba plugin) deployed to namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Check it:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "ecp-delegator-aruba.fullname" . }}

The aruba plugin writes arubacloud.com CRs: resources stay pending until the
arubacloud-resource-operator (and its Aruba credentials) is installed in this
cluster. See https://github.com/Arubacloud/arubacloud-resource-operator

The delegator requires the ECP CRDs (installed by the ecp chart, or apply
chart/crds/ from the repository).
